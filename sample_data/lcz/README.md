# Sample LCZ raster

`lcz_sofia_sample.tif` is a 24 × 24 pixel crop around the synthetic sample sensor at
42.70° N, 23.32° E. It was derived from the supplied filtered Global LCZ map version 3 and
retains its original EPSG:4326 grid and class values. It is used only by the offline tests.

Source: Demuzere et al. (2022), *Global Local Climate Zone map*, nominal year 2018,
approximately 100 m resolution, CC BY 4.0. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6364594.
